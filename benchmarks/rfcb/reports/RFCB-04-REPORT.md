## 7. Pre-Registered Correlation Protocol & Multi-Project Tracking

### Conceptual Clarification
This analysis evaluates whether the agent's internal heuristic evaluation function directionally and proportionally tracks senior human editor ratings between revision passes ($\Delta\text{Quality}_{\text{agent}} \leftrightarrow \Delta\text{Quality}_{\text{human}}$). It measures **revision heuristic sensitivity**, not objective ground-truth editorial quality.

### Formal Delta Definitions:
1. **$\Delta\text{Quality}_{\text{agent}}$**:
   $$\Delta\text{Quality}_{\text{agent}} = \text{Score}_{\text{agent}}(T_{\text{final}}) - \text{Score}_{\text{agent}}(T_{\text{initial}})$$
   where $\text{Score}_{\text{agent}} \in [0.0, 1.0]$ is evaluated by `EditQualityEvaluator` over narrative, pacing, continuity, and audio loss terms.
2. **$\Delta\text{Quality}_{\text{human}}$**:
   $$\Delta\text{Quality}_{\text{human}} = \frac{1}{10.0} \left( \overline{\text{Score}}_{\text{human}}(T_{\text{final}}) - \overline{\text{Score}}_{\text{human}}(T_{\text{initial}}) \right)$$
   where $\overline{\text{Score}}_{\text{human}} \in [1.0, 10.0]$ is the weighted aggregate score of 3 blind senior editors across all 9 uncollapsed dimensions.
