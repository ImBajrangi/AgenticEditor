### 5.2 Granular Diagnostic Case Study: The Redundant Coverage Trap
1. **The Diagnosed Failure**:
   * In the pre-intervention run, the agent inserted consecutive scenic 4K 120fps drone sweeps of the mountain ridge where kinetic rider action was required.
   * Observed effect: Story Coverage dropped to $7.10$ and Pacing Momentum dropped to $7.50$ in initial cuts.
2. **Evaluator Qualitative Evidence**:
   * *Editor 1*: *"At 00:18–00:24, the cut inserts two consecutive 4K drone sweeps of the mountain ridge right after the rock garden. They look gorgeous, but the rider is a tiny speck and we lose the physical speed and suspension drama of the root drop."*
   * *Editor 2*: *"Pacing stalls in the second third. Edit A selected a sweeping wide establishing shot where a tight helmet-cam or low-angle pass was needed to maintain kinetic acceleration into the gap jump."*
3. **Root-Cause Analysis**:
   * Initial fitness function weighted raw visual quality excessively:
     $$\text{Fitness} = \text{ProScore} - \text{ConScore} + 3.0 \cdot \text{StoryPotential} + 1.5 \cdot \text{VisualQuality}$$
   * Because the empty drone shots had $\text{VisualQuality} = 0.98$, they outscored the technically rougher GoPro footage ($\text{VisualQuality} = 0.76$, $\text{StoryPotential} = 0.88$).
4. **Algorithmic Intervention Implemented**:
   * Added `CoverageSimilarity` multi-factor redundancy penalty and scaled kinetic multipliers in action beats:
     ```typescript
     if (beat.role === "BUILD" || beat.role === "PEAK_ACTION" || beat.role === "CLIMAX") {
       const kineticMultiplier = 4.5;
       const redundancyPenalty = CoverageSimilarity.evaluate(previousShot, candidate).isRedundant ? 2.5 : 0.0;
       fitness += (candidate.storyPotential * kineticMultiplier) - redundancyPenalty;
     }
     ```
   * Following this fix, the system configuration was **permanently frozen** across all held-out projects (RFCB-02 through RFCB-20).

## 7. Pre-Registered Correlation Protocol & Multi-Project Tracking

### Conceptual Clarification
This analysis evaluates whether the agent's internal heuristic evaluation function directionally and proportionally tracks senior human editor ratings between revision passes ($\Delta\text{Quality}_{\text{agent}} \leftrightarrow \Delta\text{Quality}_{\text{human}}$). It measures **revision heuristic sensitivity**, not objective ground-truth editorial quality.

### Formal Delta Definitions:
1. **$\Delta\text{Quality}_{\text{agent}}$**:
   $$\Delta\text{Quality}_{\text{agent}} = \text{Score}_{\text{agent}}(T_{\text{final}}) - \text{Score}_{\text{agent}}(T_{\text{initial}})$$
   where $\text{Score}_{\text{agent}} \in [0.0, 1.0]$ is evaluated by `EditQualityEvaluator` over narrative, pacing, continuity, and audio loss terms.
2. **$\Delta\text{Quality}_{\text{human}}$**:
   $$\Delta\text{Quality}_{\text{human}} = \frac{1}{10.0} \left( \overline{\text{Score}}_{\text{human}}(T_{\text{final}}) - \overline{\text{Score}}_{\text{human}}(T_{\text{initial}}) \right)$$
   where $\overline{\text{Score}}_{\text{human}} \in [1.0, 10.0]$ is the weighted aggregate score of 3 blind senior editors across all 9 uncollapsed dimensions.
